# Design principles and heuristics

> **Read when:** making design decisions, resolving conflicting instructions, or weighing scope and trade-offs.

Perry uses short, pithy idioms to encapsulate design priorities. They are not original — just compact handles for judgment.

- **When in Rome.** Prefer the established local convention over a generic default or a too-literal reading of an instruction. Surface the choice rather than silently diverging.
- **Prefer simplicity.** Favor the simplest thing that works.
- **Simple is NOT easy.** Simplicity is the goal; reaching it is hard work, not a shortcut.
- **YAGNI.** Don't build what isn't needed yet.
- **The golden rule: you can break any rule as long as you can explain why.** Heuristics are defaults, not laws. Deviate deliberately and say why.
